Typefly

Type in your language. Send it in theirs.

Typefly is an iPhone keyboard with AI translation built in. Type in your own language, tap a language, and your whole message is translated before you send it. Copy a message in another language and its meaning appears right above the keyboard.

Get started

  1. Add the keyboardSettings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard… › Typefly. Then tap Typefly and turn on Allow Full Access. In the Typefly app, tap Settings to jump straight there.
  2. Choose an AI serviceIn the app, open AI Service. Add an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek or another provider, or sign in with your ChatGPT plan (experimental). Use Try it to check the connection.
  3. Start chattingIn any chat app, press and hold 🌐 and switch to Typefly.

How to use it

✍️

Reply in their language

Type as usual, then tap a language at the top of the keyboard. Your whole message is translated. Not happy with it? Tap Undo.

💬

Understand their message

Press and hold their message › Copy. A “Them” bubble shows what it means, and your reply language switches to theirs.

📷

Translate screenshots

Select text › Share › Typefly Translate. For text you can't select, take a screenshot and share it. Text is recognized on your iPhone.

Questions

Why does Typefly ask for Full Access?

iOS keyboards need Full Access to use the internet. Typefly uses it only to send the text you translate to your AI service, and to read a message you copy. Typing works without it. Typefly has no servers and never collects what you type.

Translation doesn't work

Open the Typefly app › AI Service, check that a key is filled in or ChatGPT is connected, and tap Try it. Then make sure Allow Full Access is on in Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Typefly.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free. Translation runs on your own AI provider account: the provider charges you for API usage at its own prices, or you use your ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan. Typing itself works offline and costs nothing.

A copied message doesn't show up

Tap the clipboard icon at the top right of the keyboard. In the app, check that Translate copied messages automatically is on.

Typefly Translate isn't in the Share menu

In the Share menu, scroll to the end of the app row, tap More, and add Typefly Translate.

Which languages are supported?

You can type in Chinese (pinyin), English and Japanese (romaji). Translation covers English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, French, German, Spanish, Russian, Thai and Vietnamese.
