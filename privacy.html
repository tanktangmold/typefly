Typefly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: Typefly has no servers and does not collect your data. Text you choose to translate goes straight from your iPhone to the AI service you picked.

  1. We do not collect your data. Typefly has no servers. We do not collect, store or share anything you type, and we do not track you. The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking code.
  2. Typing happens on your device. Pinyin and romaji input, dictionaries, suggestions and typo correction run entirely on your iPhone, without network access.
  3. Text you translate is sent to the AI provider you choose. Only when you tap a language to translate, or when a copied message is translated automatically, that text is sent directly from your device to the provider you selected in the app: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, Alibaba Cloud, Moonshot AI, Zhipu AI, ByteDance (Volcano Engine), Baidu, SiliconFlow, xAI, Mistral AI, Groq, OpenRouter, or a service you enter yourself. The provider processes it under its own privacy policy. The app asks for your consent first, and you can withdraw it at any time on the AI Service screen.
  4. Clipboard. With Translate copied messages automatically turned on, the keyboard reads text you just copied, only while the keyboard is visible, to show a translation. Clipboard contents are not stored.
  5. Screenshots. Text in screenshots shared to Typefly Translate is recognized on your device. Images are never uploaded. Recognized text is sent for translation as described in section 3.
  6. API keys and sign-ins. API keys and your ChatGPT sign-in are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain and used only to call the provider you chose.
  7. Full Access. Full Access is used only for network access for translation and to read messages you copy. Typing works without it.
  8. Children. The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect their information.
  9. Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
